Blog, pages, and other templates

Blog

The Blog section lists your posts. The first post on the first page is shown large. Each post shows its date, topic (its first tag), reading time, author, number of comments, title, summary, and featured image.

SettingWhat it does
LayoutStacked, or Headings in the margin to put month names in a left column on desktop.
TextShown under the blog title.
Group posts by monthAdds a month heading between posts.
Show featured imagesShows each post's featured image.
Posts per pageFrom 6 to 24.

Tags you add to posts appear as topics at the top of the blog, so customers can browse by topic.

Article

The Article section shows the post with its date, author, topics, reading time, featured image (with its alt text as the caption), and content.

SettingWhat it does
LayoutStacked, or Headings in the margin to put the title and date in a column beside the image on desktop.
Products in this postProducts shown next to the post. Connect a metafield to show different products on each post.

To show different products on each post, create a blog post metafield of the Product type with the list option (in Settings > Custom data), fill it in on each post, and connect Products in this post to it. See Product metafields.

Comments follow the settings of each blog in Online Store > Blog posts > Manage comments. When comments need approval, customers see a message that their comment is waiting for review.

Pages

The Default page template shows the page title and content. The Page section has these settings, all optional:

SettingWhat it does
LayoutStacked, or Headings in the margin to put the headings of your page content in a left column on desktop and set quotes as pull quotes.
ImageA portrait image beside the content on desktop, and under the title on mobile.
Image captionA line under the image.
Sign-offA line under the content, such as the names of the people behind the shop.
Sign-off detailA second, smaller line under the sign-off.

Contact page

The contact template has three sections: the page content, an FAQ, and a Contact form. To use it:

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Pages and create a page, such as "Help".
  2. Under Theme template, choose contact.
  3. In the theme editor, open the page template and edit the questions and the text beside the form.

See Home page sections for the FAQ and Contact form settings.

404 page

Shown when a page doesn't exist. It has a search box, a link to the home page, and a few collections.

SettingWhat it does
Heading and TextThe message.
Show collectionsShows up to three collections that have products.
CollectionsLeave blank to show the first three collections that have products.

The Kennel and Soot presets add a Featured products section below.

Password page

Shown while your store is password protected. It has your logo or store name, a heading, your password page message, an email signup form, and a link to enter the store with the password.

SettingWhat it does
HeadingLarge heading, such as "Opening soon".
Image and Image positionAn optional image on the Left or Right.

The text under the heading is the password page message in Online Store > Preferences.

Gift card page

Customers who receive a gift card open this page from their email. It shows the balance, the code, a QR code, an Apple Wallet button on supported devices, and buttons to copy the code and print the card. Expired or used cards say so and hide the code.

Choose the card's colors with Theme settings > Colors > Gift card color scheme.
