Collections, search, and cart

Collection page

In the theme editor, choose Collections > Default collection and select the Collection section. The page shows the collection title, description, and image, then the products.

Product cards

Each card is one link to the product: the image on a frame tinted with the product's color, how long the product lasts (see Days of supply on product cards), the title, the price, and the plan price when the product has delivery plans. Cards show compare-at prices, "Sold out", and unit prices. When a product has custom.facts or custom.notes, they show under the title. Cards have no badges or buttons.

Product groups

The collection page can split products into groups, each with its own heading. Groups show only when:

  • the collection is sorted manually (or the customer has not chosen a sort order),
  • no filter is applied,
  • and every product fits on one page.

Otherwise, the page shows a plain grid. Where products come from:

  1. Product group blocks (up to 8). Enter a Product tag: products with that tag form the group. Add a Heading, a Subheading (start it with a number, such as "6–10 am", to show the number large), a Group note, and a Color scheme for the group's band. A product goes into the first group whose tag it has.
  2. Without matching tags, Grouped by date groups products by custom.made_on, newest first.
  3. Otherwise, products are grouped by product type.

Products that fit no group are listed last, under Heading for other products.

Variant filter

Add the Variant filter block and enter an Option name, such as "Dog size". Customers pick a value once, and every card shows that variant's price, image, and days of supply. It changes the variant shown on the cards. It does not filter out products or change the cart.

Filters

Filters come from Shopify's free Search & Discovery app:

  1. Install Search & Discovery from the Shopify App Store.
  2. In the app, open Filters and add the filters you want, such as availability, price, product type, variant options (for example "Dog size"), or product metafields (for example a roast level).
  3. In the theme editor, make sure Enable filtering is on in the Collection and Search sections.

Customers can combine filters. Active filters show as removable buttons above the products.

The same app sets up Complementary products for the Product recommendations section: open Product recommendations in the app, choose a product, and add the products that go with it.

Search

The Search section shows products, then blog posts and pages, with the same filters and sorting as collection pages. Settings: Products per page, Enable filtering, Enable sorting, Filter layout, Card size, and Image ratio. It also accepts a Variant filter block.

Under No results:

  • Suggested searches: words to suggest, separated by commas.
  • Products for no results: a collection to show. Leave blank to show every product.
  • Collections for no results: collections to suggest. Leave blank to show the first collections with products.

Cart

Theme settings > Cart > Cart type chooses between:

  • Drawer: adding a product opens a panel over the current page. Customers can change quantities, remove items, add a note, and check out without leaving the page.
  • Page: adding a product goes to the cart page.

Both show, for each item: image, title, options, pack size, plan name or "One-time purchase", days of supply, unit price, discounts, and quantity. Below the items: discounts on the order, subtotal, the tax note, the cart note (turn it off with Show cart note), the checkout button, and dynamic checkout buttons.

When the cart has items on a delivery plan, they are grouped under "On repeat" and the rest under "Once". The summary shows what customers pay today, and a "Then" note says when each plan item comes again and what it costs.

Discount codes are entered at checkout. The cart shows automatic discounts and discounts that are already applied.
