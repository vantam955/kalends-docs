FAQ and troubleshooting

Delivery plans and dates

Why don't delivery plans show on my product page?

The product has no selling plans. Plans come from a subscriptions app, such as Shopify Subscriptions. Add the product, or the selected variant, to a plan in the app. The Purchase options block then lists the plans. If you removed the Purchase options block from the Product section, add it back.

Why is a plan group grayed out?

The selected variant isn't part of that plan group. For example, a trial size may be sold only as a one-time purchase. Add the variant to the plan in your subscriptions app, or leave it out on purpose.

Why don't I see any ship dates?

Check each of these:

  • At least one day is checked under Theme settings > Delivery dates > Shipping days.
  • You are browsing in your store's own country. Dates are hidden for other countries, because your shipping days are for your home warehouse.
  • For the Delivery dates block: the product has delivery plans and a plan is selected.
  • JavaScript is on. Without it, the text shows without a date.

Do the ship dates change orders or checkout?

No. Dates are estimates calculated from Shipping days and Same-day cutoff. They don't change orders, shipping rates, checkout, or the delivery dates in your subscriptions app. Keep them in line with Settings > Shipping and delivery.

Why are the later deliveries or the "Then" note missing?

The theme couldn't read the plan's frequency from its name. Add a line such as Subscribe and save = 1 month to Theme settings > Delivery dates > Delivery frequencies. See Delivery plans and dates.

Why does the card suggest a different plan than I expected?

Cards suggest the longest plan that doesn't outlast the product, based on custom.days_of_supply. Check the number of days on the product and its variants.

Product details

How do I show how long a product lasts?

Create the custom.days_of_supply metafield with the Integer type and enter the number of days one unit lasts. No theme setting is needed. To choose how cards show it, use Theme settings > Style > Days of supply on product cards. See Product metafields.

I added a metafield, but nothing shows. Why?

Check that the namespace and key match exactly, such as custom.days_of_supply and not custom.days_supply, and that the type matches the table in Product metafields. When a variant has its own value, the variant's value is shown instead of the product's.

How do I show a roast or batch date?

Create a Date metafield, such as custom.made_on. Add the Made-on date block to the Product section and connect its Date setting to the metafield. The Soot preset already has the block.

Why does every product show the same ingredients text?

A Text block's text belongs to the template, so every product shares it. Connect the block's Text setting to a Multi-line text metafield and fill in the metafield on each product. See Product metafields.

How do I change a product's color?

Set the custom.label_color metafield (Color) on the product. Cards, the product page, and the cart use it automatically. Without it, image frames use the color scheme's Selected row color.

Collections and navigation

Why aren't my products grouped on the collection page?

Groups show only when the collection is sorted manually, no filter is applied, and every product fits on one page. Also check that your Product group blocks use tags that your products have. See Collections, search, and cart.

How do I set up collection filters?

Install the free Search & Discovery app and add filters there. The theme shows them on collection and search pages when Enable filtering is on.

Why does the "Goes with it" section not show?

It uses Complementary products, which you set up per product in the Search & Discovery app. Until you add some, the section stays hidden.

Why does my menu show "More"?

The top-level items don't fit on one line at that screen width. Shorten the item titles or move some links into submenus.

Where are the country and language selectors?

They appear in the footer when your store sells in more than one country (Settings > Markets) or more than one language (Settings > Languages). With one country and one language, nothing is shown.

Why don't I see the Follow on Shop button?

It requires the Shop sales channel. Install it, then turn on Show Follow on Shop in the Footer settings.

Design

Why doesn't the corner radius round every line?

By design, Corner radius rounds buttons, option buttons, inputs, and images. Lines, plan options, and section backgrounds stay square.

How do I change the colors?

Go to Theme settings > Colors and edit a color scheme. Every section that uses the scheme updates. To give one section different colors, change its Color scheme setting.

Can I use a different font?

Yes. Theme settings > Typography uses Shopify's font library. Custom font files are not supported.

Can I switch to another preset?

Each preset installs as its own theme from its Theme Store listing. You can also match a preset by changing the settings it uses. See Choose a preset.

Apps, languages, and updates

Can I add an app block?

Yes. The Product and Cart sections accept app blocks. Select the section, click Add block, and choose the app's block. Apps that need code changes are not supported by our support team.

How do I translate the theme?

Use Settings > Languages and the Translate & Adapt app. All theme text can be translated.

How do I update the theme?

When a new version is available, you see a notice in Online Store > Themes. Updates keep your settings. If you edited the theme code, the update is added to your theme library as a new copy so you can move your changes.
