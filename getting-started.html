Getting started with Kalends

Kalends is built for brands with a small lineup that customers buy again and again: tea, coffee, body care refills, pet food. Product pages are organized around how long a product lasts and when the next delivery leaves. This guide takes you from installation to a store that looks like the demo.

1. Install and publish

  1. In the Shopify Theme Store, open the preset you want and click Try theme or Buy theme. See Choose a preset for the differences between Kalends, Kennel, and Soot.
  2. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes. The theme appears in your theme library.
  3. Click Customize to open the theme editor. Make your changes, then click Publish when you are ready.

The preset's layout, colors, and fonts come with the theme. Demo products, images, and text do not, so the first preview uses your own products. Sections that need content you have not added yet show a short hint in the theme editor and stay hidden in your store.

2. Set up your products

The theme works with any catalog, but it is designed for 2–10 products. For each product:

  • Add product details. Title, description, images (with alt text), price, and variants work as in any theme. To show captions under product page images, use the custom.captions metafield.
  • Add how long it lasts (recommended). Create the custom.days_of_supply metafield (Integer) and enter the number of days one unit lasts. Cards, the product page, and the cart use it. See Product metafields.
  • Add delivery plans (optional). Install a subscriptions app such as Shopify Subscriptions and add products to a plan. The product page lists every plan, with "One-time purchase" selected by default. See Delivery plans and dates.
  • Add more details (optional). Metafields such as custom.pack ("30 scoops · 180 g"), custom.subtitle, custom.made_on, and custom.notes add lines to cards and product pages. Every one of them is optional: when it is empty, the line is hidden.

3. Enter your shipping days

Go to Theme settings > Delivery dates, check the days you ship orders, and choose your Same-day cutoff. The theme then shows lines such as "Ships Monday to Friday. Orders placed by 2:00 pm ship the same day." and "First box ships about Wed, Oct 14".

These dates are for display only. They are estimates calculated from the days you enter here, and they do not change orders, shipping rates, or checkout. Match them to your shipping settings in Settings > Shipping and delivery. See Delivery plans and dates.

4. Choose colors and fonts

Go to Theme settings:

  • Colors. Each preset comes with four color schemes, and you can edit them or add more. Each section has a Color scheme setting, so changing a scheme updates every section that uses it. See Theme settings.
  • Typography. Pick a Heading font and a Body font, and choose whether navigation, buttons, and prices use the heading or the body font.
  • Style. Set the Corner radius and how Days of supply on product cards appear.

5. Build your home page

Each preset comes with its own home page. You can keep it, reorder sections, or add others from the section picker. The sections are described in Home page sections. Most sections fill themselves with your products and collections until you choose specific ones.

6. Menus, header, and footer

  • Header. Choose the Menu (Main menu by default) and the Customer account menu. Upload a logo in Theme settings > Logo, or keep your store name. See Header and footer.
  • Footer. Add blocks for email signup, menus, text, and your shipping schedule. Add social media links in Theme settings > Social media.

7. Before you launch

  • Add a favicon in Theme settings > Logo > Favicon (32 x 32px .png recommended).
  • Set up filters in the Search & Discovery app. See Collections, search, and cart.
  • Create a contact page: add a page and choose the contact template in Theme template. See Blog, pages, and other templates.
  • Preview the store on your phone and check your images and text.
