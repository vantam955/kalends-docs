Header and footer

Header

In the theme editor, select Header at the top of the page list.

SettingWhat it does
MenuThe menu shown in the header. Default: Main menu.
Customer account menuLinks in the account panel that opens from the account icon. Default: Customer account main menu.
LayoutTwo rows, One row, or Centered. Applies on screens 1024px and wider. On smaller screens, navigation opens from the menu button.
Color schemeColors of the header, its menu panels, and the search box.

The logo and its width are set in Theme settings > Logo. Without a logo, the store name is shown in the heading font.

Menu

  • Create and edit menus in Online Store > Navigation. Menus can have up to three levels.
  • On screens 1024px and wider, top-level items show in one line. An item with a submenu opens a full-width panel: second-level items become column headings and third-level items are listed below them. When the items don't fit on one line, the last ones move into a "More" item.
  • On smaller screens, the menu opens from the menu button.

Search, account, and cart

  • The search icon suggests products, collections, pages, and blog posts as customers type. Pressing Enter opens the search page.
  • The account icon appears when customer accounts are turned on in Settings > Customer accounts. It uses Shopify's account panel, so customers can sign in without leaving the page.
  • The cart icon shows the number of items and opens the cart drawer or the cart page, depending on Theme settings > Cart > Cart type.

When the cart has an item on a delivery plan and you have set shipping days, the header also shows "First box ships about Oct 14", linking to the cart summary. See Delivery plans and dates.

When customers scroll

The header stays at the top of the screen. While scrolling, it shrinks to one compact row so it takes less space. This is built in and has no setting.

Footer

Select Footer at the bottom of the page list.

SettingWhat it does
Color schemeFooter colors. Every preset uses a dark scheme by default.
Show country/region selectorShown when your store sells in more than one country. Add countries in Settings > Markets.
Show language selectorShown when your store has more than one language. Add languages in Settings > Languages.
Show Follow on ShopThe Follow on Shop button. Requires the Shop sales channel.
Show payment iconsIcons for the payment methods enabled on your store.

Social media icons appear for the accounts you add in Theme settings > Social media.

Footer blocks

Add, remove, and reorder blocks:

  • Email signup (one per footer): Heading and Text. Customers who sign up are added to your customer list with email marketing consent.
  • Menu: Heading and Menu. Pick any menu from Online Store > Navigation.
  • Text: Heading and Text, for a short description of your store.
  • Shipping schedule (one per footer): your shipping days, with an optional next ship date. Hidden until you choose at least one shipping day. See Delivery plans and dates.

The bottom line shows the copyright with your store name, the "Powered by Shopify" link, links to your store policies, and the payment icons.
