Product metafields

Metafields are extra fields you add to products, such as how many days a product lasts or the date a batch was made. Kalends reads a few product metafields on its own, with no theme editor setup. Every one is optional: when a metafield is empty or missing, the line that uses it is hidden and the rest of the page stays the same.

Metafields the theme reads

Use exactly these namespaces, keys, and types. The theme does not read other names.

Namespace and keyTypeWhere it shows
custom.days_of_supplyIntegerHow many days one unit lasts. Product cards (see Days of supply on product cards), the product page, Product list rows, and cart lines. Also used to suggest the delivery plan that fits best. Can be set on products and on variants.
custom.label_colorColorThe product's own color. A light tint of it fills the frame behind the product's images on cards, the product page, the cart, and search suggestions.
custom.packSingle line textPack size, such as "30 scoops · 180 g". Under the title on the product page, on Product list rows, and on cart lines.
custom.subtitleSingle line textA short line under the product title, such as "Chicken, oats and pumpkin". On the product page and in Featured products with the Menu layout, where it replaces the product type.
custom.time_of_useSingle line textA time of day, such as "7:00 am". In the Three columns product layout it replaces the product type in the margin, and it starts the How to use block.
custom.how_to_useMulti-line textDirections shown by the How to use block.
custom.careMulti-line textCare and storage rows shown by the How to use block. Write one row per line, with a name before a colon, such as Once opened: Keep the lid on.
custom.made_onDateThe date a batch was made, roasted, or baked. Groups products in the Grouped by date layouts, shows in the Made-on date block when you connect it, and dates rows in the Collections grid with one column.
custom.factsSingle line text, list of valuesShort facts, such as "Washed", "Light roast", "340 g". Shown under the title on cards, list rows, and cart lines.
custom.notesSingle line text, list of valuesTasting or scent notes, such as "Bergamot", "Apricot". Shown under the title on the product page, cards, and list rows.
custom.captionsSingle line text, list of valuesCaptions for the product page images, in the same order as the product's media: the first value is the first image's caption. Images after the last value have no caption.

Days of supply on variants

When variants last for different times, such as a 2 kg and a 12 kg bag, create custom.days_of_supply for variants too. A variant's value wins over the product's value, and the product page updates when a customer picks another variant. Products whose variants all last the same time only need the product metafield.

The number is per unit and does not change with the quantity in the cart.

Create a metafield definition

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Settings > Custom data.
  2. Choose Products (or Variants for variant values).
  3. Click Add definition.
  4. Enter a name, such as "Days of supply". In the namespace and key field, enter the exact value from the table, such as custom.days_of_supply.
  5. Select the type from the table. For custom.facts, custom.notes, and custom.captions, choose Single line text and then the list of values option.
  6. Click Save.

Then open a product in Products and fill in the value in its metafields area. Values save with the product.

Connect a metafield to a block

Some blocks show different content on each product only when you connect them to a metafield. This is called a dynamic source.

  1. In the theme editor, open Products > Default product and select the block.
  2. Next to the setting, click the Connect dynamic source icon.
  3. Choose the metafield. The editor only lists metafields whose type fits the setting.

Useful connections:

BlockSettingMetafield to connect
Made-on dateDateA Date metafield, such as custom.made_on. The block stays hidden on products without a date.
TextTextA Multi-line text metafield, such as an ingredients list you name custom.ingredients.
SpecificationsRowsA Multi-line text metafield with one row per line, such as Protein: 26%.
Collapsible rowTextA Multi-line text metafield, such as feeding notes.
Article sectionProducts in this postA blog post metafield of the Product type, with the list option, so each post shows its own products.

Metafields you connect yourself can use any name. Only the metafields in the first table are read by the theme without a connection.

What happens without metafields

  • No custom.days_of_supply: cards, rows, and the product page leave out the ruler or sentence. The plan suggestion on cards uses the first plan the theme can read instead.
  • No custom.label_color: image frames use the color scheme's Selected row color.
  • No custom.made_on: Grouped by date layouts group products by product type, and the Made-on date block is hidden.
  • No custom.facts or custom.notes: cards show the title and price only.
