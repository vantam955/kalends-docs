Choose a preset

Kalends comes in three presets. They share the same sections, blocks, and settings, so anything you can do in one preset you can do in the others. What changes is the starting point: fonts, colors, corner radius, and how the main pages are laid out.

Kalends

The home page opens with an Image banner (Image beside text) that features one product and a Plan example of how a delivery plan works. A Product list follows, with your products grouped as a price list, then a Product gallery and Blog posts.

On the product page (Three columns), the product's time of use or product type sits in a narrow margin next to the photos, and a ruler under the price shows how many days the product lasts. Descriptions, text blocks, and collapsible rows run full width below.

The collection page uses Groups in the margin: add Product group blocks with a product tag, and each group becomes a band with its heading in the left margin.

Kennel

The home page opens with a text-only Image banner, followed by a Product gallery, a Featured products section with the Menu layout and a Variant filter for dog size, an Image with text, and Blog posts as a list.

On the product page (Centered title), the title and product type are centered above the photos and the buying column. When a product has a "Dog size" option, a band above the options shows the selected size and bag, and each option row shows how many days that size lasts. A Product details section with columns holds ingredients, specifications, and care.

The collection and search pages use a Variant filter: customers pick a dog size once, and every card shows that size's price and days of supply.

Soot

The home page opens with an Image banner in the Two pages layout, followed by a Product list with the Two pages layout, an Image with text, and a Product gallery.

On the product page (Edge-to-edge image), the first photo runs to the left edge of the screen. A Made-on date block shows the roast date from the custom.made_on metafield, and the custom.notes metafield shows tasting notes under the title.

The collection page uses Grouped by date: products are grouped by their custom.made_on date, newest first. Products without a date are grouped by product type. Blog, article, FAQ, and contact pages put their headings in the left margin.

Switching presets

Presets are styles of the same theme. To try another one, open its listing in the Theme Store and add it to your theme library; it installs as a separate theme with that preset's settings. Your products, collections, and metafields stay the same. Settings you changed in one theme are not copied to the other.

You can also recreate a preset by hand: every difference above is a setting you can change in the theme editor, such as Product layout in the Product section or Layout in the Collection section.
