Product page

Every part of the product page is a block that you can reorder, remove, or add. In the theme editor, choose Products > Default product from the page menu at the top, then select the Product section.

Product section

SettingWhat it does
Product layoutTimeline (used by every preset), Three columns, Centered title, or Edge-to-edge image. See below.
Image ratioSquare, Portrait, or Landscape frames for product images. Images are cropped to fit.
Timeline marksHow the Timeline layout draws its marks: Ruler (a ruled line with ticks), Bold numbers, or Labels (each mark in a small block).

Layouts

  • Timeline. The photos and their numbered thumbnails sit on the left, and the buying column stays in view on the right while customers scroll. Below the photos, each content block is a row with a mark beside it that says when it happens, counted from the order:

    • the date from the Made-on date block, such as "Roasted Oct 12";
    • "Ships about Oct 19", from the shipping days in Theme settings > Delivery dates;
    • "Runs out about Day 30", from custom.days_of_supply on the selected variant or the product;
    • with a delivery plan selected, "Box 2 ships about Day 30" and "Then every month". When the box runs out

    within 3 days of the next delivery, the two marks become one. Later deliveries are always shown relative to the first ("about a month later"), never as a date.

    These delivery marks go in the row of the Delivery dates block; without that block, they go just before the questions. Add a Timeline mark block before any content block to give that row your own mark, such as "Settles about · Day 5". Only the "Ships about" date uses the color scheme's date color. A short schedule line under the buy buttons repeats the ship date and the next delivery. When a product has no dates at all (no Made-on date, shipping days, days of supply, delivery plans or Timeline marks), the rows are numbered I, II, III instead.

  • Three columns. A narrow margin on the left shows the product's time of use (custom.time_of_use) or its product type, with numbered thumbnails. The photo sits in the middle and the buying column on the right. A ruler under the price shows how long the product lasts (custom.days_of_supply). Description, text, How to use, and collapsible row blocks show full width below the photos. Collapsible rows that follow each other are gathered under one "Questions" heading.
  • Centered title. The product type and title are centered above a large first photo, with the other photos in a two-column grid. Each option row that has its own price also shows how many days that variant lasts. Text blocks show at the end of the buying column. When the product has a "Dog size" option, a band above the options shows the selected size and pack.
  • Edge-to-edge image. The first photo runs to the left edge of the screen, with the other photos in three columns below. Description and text blocks show under the photos. Use the Made-on date block to show a batch date in the buying column.

The block settings below are the same in every layout. Only their position changes. Specifications and Timeline mark blocks show only in the Timeline layout.

Product details section

A second section for longer content under the product. The presets now put this content in the Product section (Timeline layout), so it sits beside the buying column.

SettingWhat it does
HeadingOptional heading above the blocks.
LayoutRows stacks blocks with their headings in a left column. Columns puts blocks side by side.

Blocks: Text, Specifications (Heading and Rows: one row per line, with the name and value separated by a colon, such as Protein: 26%), Description, and Collapsible row.

Media

The product page supports images, videos, YouTube and Vimeo videos, and 3D models (with AR on supported phones). When a customer picks a variant with its own image, that image moves to the front. Thumbnails are numbered. To add captions, fill the custom.captions metafield (see Product metafields); alt text is read by screen readers and is never shown as a caption. Images without a caption have none.

Color swatches

The variant picker shows a swatch next to an option value when the value has a color or image:

  1. In Products, open a product and choose a Category.
  2. Under Category metafields, add a metafield such as Color, and pick or create its values.
  3. In Variants, edit the option and connect it to that metafield.
  4. In Content > Metaobjects, open each value and set its color, or upload an image.

Values without a color or image show as regular options. To hide all swatches, turn off Show swatches in the Variant picker block.

Product color

Create the custom.label_color metafield (Color) and set it on each product. A light tint of that color fills the frame behind the product's images on the product page, cards, the cart, and search suggestions. No theme editor setup is needed. See Product metafields.
