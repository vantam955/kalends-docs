Home page sections

Add a section with Add section in the theme editor. These sections work on the home page and on any other page template. Most of them fill themselves with your products and collections until you choose specific ones, so a new store never shows empty boxes.

Every section below has a Color scheme setting. See Theme settings for color schemes.

Image banner

The first screen of the home page: a large heading, an image, and a short introduction to one product.

SettingWhat it does
LayoutImage beside text, Text over image, Text only, or Two pages (text on a left page, image and product on a right page).
Panel color schemeThe panel behind the text, used with Text over image.
HeadingLeave blank to use the store name.
Heading sizeMedium, Large, or Extra large.
TextA short introduction.
ImageLeave blank to use the first image of the product in the Product block.
Image ratio and Image ratio on mobileFrame shape on large and small screens.
Image captionOptional caption under the image.

Blocks:

  • Small text (up to 3): short lines above the heading, such as "Small batch", "Ships Monday to Friday".
  • Product: a summary of one product with a link to its page. Leave the product blank to use the first product with an image. The banner has no Add to cart button.
  • Buttons: First button label, First button link, Second button label, Second button link.
  • Plan example: shows how a delivery plan works for one product: plan price, regular price, and how often it arrives. Heading, Text, Product (blank uses the product above). Shown only when the product has delivery plans. No calendar dates are used.
  • Shipping schedule: your shipping days and next ship date. See Delivery plans and dates.

Product list

A price list of your products, grouped by collection. Each row has a small image, the name, a dotted line, and the price.

SettingWhat it does
HeadingSection heading.
LayoutList shows groups one after another. Two pages shows two groups side by side like an open book.

Blocks:

  • Collection (up to 6): one group. Collection, Subheading (start with a number, such as "6–10 am", to show the number large), Products (leave blank to use the collection's products), and Color scheme for the group's band.
  • Specifications (up to 2): a short table of name and value rows, placed after the group before it. Heading and Rows (one row per line, with the name and value separated by a colon).

With no collection chosen, products are grouped by collection, or by product type with Two pages.

Delivery plan timeline

One product with delivery plans, its photo, and the same timeline marks as the product page's Timeline layout: "Ships about Oct 19" (from your shipping days), "Box 2 ships about Day 30", "Runs out about Day 30" (from custom.days_of_supply) and "Then every month". Customers can pick a delivery rhythm to see the marks change; this choice is only for viewing and doesn't add anything to the cart. Later deliveries are shown relative to the first, never as a date. Used by the Kennel preset.

SettingWhat it does
ProductLeave blank to use the first product with delivery plans.
HeadingLeave blank to use "How a delivery plan runs".
TextOptional text under the heading.
Color schemeColors for the section.

The section is hidden when the product has no delivery plan with a frequency the theme can read.

Featured collections

Two to four collections side by side, each with a few of its products.

SettingWhat it does
HeadingSection heading.

Blocks:

  • Collection (up to 4): Collection, Subheading, Products (leave blank to use the collection's products), Color scheme.
  • Note (up to 2): a column of text, such as a delivery note. Heading, Text, Color scheme.

With no collection chosen, the first three collections with products are shown.

FAQ

SettingWhat it does

LayoutStacked, or Headings in the margin to put group headings in a left column on desktop.

Contact form

A contact form with name, email, and message fields. Messages are sent to your store's contact email.

SettingWhat it does
Heading and TextAbove the form.
Show phone number fieldAdds an optional phone field.
LayoutStacked or Headings in the margin.

Blocks, up to two, in the column beside the form:

  • Text (Heading and Text), such as what to include in a message. A blank heading shows your store name.
  • Specifications (Heading and Rows), such as reply hours. Write one row per paragraph or line, with the name and value separated by a colon, for example "Monday to Friday: 9:00 am – 5:00 pm".
