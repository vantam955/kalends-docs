Theme settings

Open the theme editor and click the gear icon to see Theme settings. These settings apply to the whole store. Section and block settings are described on the other pages of this documentation.

Logo

SettingWhat it does
LogoShown in the header and on the password page. Without a logo, the store name is shown in the heading font.
Logo widthWidth on screens 1024px and wider, from 80 to 320px.
Logo width on mobileWidth on smaller screens, from 60 to 200px.
FaviconThe small icon in browser tabs. 32 x 32px .png recommended.

Typography

SettingWhat it does
Heading fontFont for headings and product titles. Choose from Shopify's font library.
Body fontFont for paragraphs, descriptions, and form fields.
Font for navigation, buttons and pricesChoose Heading font or Body font for menus, buttons, prices, and other small interface text.
Heading size scaleMakes headings larger, from 100% to 150%. Body text stays the same.
Body text size scaleMakes body text larger, from 100% to 130%.

Each preset sets these to match its design. See Choose a preset.

Colors

Color schemes

A color scheme is a set of colors that a section uses together. Each preset comes with four schemes. You can edit them, or add more with Add scheme. Every section and some blocks have a Color scheme setting that picks one of them, so editing a scheme updates every place that uses it.

Each scheme has these colors:

ColorUsed for
BackgroundSection background.
TextHeadings, body text, links, icons, and thin lines that need to stand out, such as the ruler on product cards.
Secondary textPrices you compare against, captions, dates, and other quieter text.
LinesDividers, borders, and the dotted lines between names and prices.
Selected rowBackground of the selected purchase option, the highlighted search suggestion, disabled buttons, and the frame behind product images when a product has no custom.label_color metafield.
Delivery date textThe ship date line under the purchase options on the product page.
ButtonPrimary button background.
Button labelText on primary buttons.

Secondary buttons use the scheme's Background and Text colors with a text-colored border.

Keep enough contrast between Background and Text, and between Button and Button label. The theme does not change colors you choose.

Gift card color scheme

Gift card color scheme sets the colors of the printed card on the gift card page that customers receive by email.

Style

SettingWhat it does
Corner radiusRounds buttons, option buttons, inputs, and images, from 0 to 16px. Lines, plan options, and section backgrounds stay square.
Days of supply on product cardsHow cards show the custom.days_of_supply metafield: Ruler (a short ruler with the number of days), Sentence ("Lasts about 30 days."), or Hidden. The same choice applies to cart lines.

With Sentence, the line can start with the pack size, such as "6 kg bag.", when the variant has a unit price measurement and Shipment name is Box or Bag. When the product has a "Dog size" option, the sentence names the size: "Lasts a medium dog about 24 days."

Delivery dates

These settings turn on the ship dates shown on product pages, in the cart, and in the header. Read Delivery plans and dates for the full picture.

SettingWhat it does
Shipping daysCheck each day of the week you ship orders: Monday to Sunday. Ship dates appear only after you check at least one day.
Same-day cutoffOrders placed before this time, in your store's time zone, ship the same day. Choose a time from 9:00 am to 6:00 pm, or No same-day shipping.
Shipment nameThe word used for one shipment: Box, Bag, or Delivery. For example, "First box ships about Oct 14".
Delivery frequenciesOne plan per line, such as Deliver every month = 1 month. Use it only when the theme can't read a plan's frequency from its name.

Match these days to your shipping settings in Settings > Shipping and delivery. Dates are estimates shown to customers. They don't change orders, shipping rates, or checkout.
